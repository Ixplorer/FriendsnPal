FriendnPal & OurPadi — Data Structure Specification

Comprehensive catalog of entities remembered by the OurPadi mobile client and the FriendnPal Clinician Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) Dashboard, including peer support communities, motivational checklists, mindfulness exercises, ASMR soundscapes, journals, and mood check-ins.

1 Patient Profile (User)
Mobile Client Profile

The individual using OurPadi (student, corporate employee, addiction recovery seeker, or nocturnal Upwork freelancer like Amina).

Why store it: To authenticate across mobile and web platforms, personalize AI tone, assign to clinical cohorts and peer communities, and track subscription standing.

Field NameData TypeDescription & Example
user_idUUIDUnique primary key (e.g. usr_8241a7c2).
client_platformEnumOURPADI_IOS, OURPADI_ANDROID, WHATSAPP_AUXILIARY.
phone_numberStringInternational mobile number (e.g. +2348012345678).
emailStringUser contact email.
display_nameStringPreferred pseudonym or community alias (e.g. "Amina").
preferred_languageEnumNIGERIAN_ENGLISH, NIGERIAN_PIDGIN.
city_locationStringPrimary city location (e.g. "Garki, Abuja").
occupation_categoryEnumREMOTE_FREELANCER, CORPORATE_EMPLOYEE, STUDENT.
joined_community_idsArray[UUID]IDs of joined peer communities.
assigned_clinician_idUUIDForeign key to managing Clinician on RPM Dashboard.
current_risk_tierEnumNORMAL, MODERATE_AT_RISK, HIGH_RISK, CRITICAL.
account_statusEnumTRIAL, ACTIVE_SUBSCRIBER, PAST_DUE, CRISIS_SUSPENDED.
2 Peer Support Community (CommunityGroup)
Taskbar Communities

A moderated anonymous peer support community accessible from the bottom taskbar.

Why store it: Implements FR-12 to provide safe spaces for shared lived experience (Parental Abuse, Addiction, Student Pressure, etc.).

Field NameData TypeDescription & Example
community_idUUIDUnique community identifier (e.g. com_parental_abuse).
titleString"Parental Abuse Support Group", "Addiction & Recovery Support", "Student Stress & Exams".
descriptionTextSafety guidelines, community purpose, and ground rules.
categoryEnumTRAUMA, SUBSTANCE_RECOVERY, ACADEMIC, OCCUPATIONAL.
member_countIntegerTotal active anonymous participants.
is_anonymous_posting_enforcedBooleanTrue to mandate anonymous screen aliases.
3 Community Post & Reply (CommunityPost)
Peer Content

An anonymous discussion post or supportive reply shared within a peer support group.

Why store it: Allows users to share coping stories and read empathetic responses without judgment.

Field NameData TypeDescription & Example
post_idUUIDUnique post identifier.
community_idUUIDForeign key to CommunityGroup.
author_aliasStringAnonymous screen alias (e.g. "Warrior94").
post_contentTextNarrative body text.
support_reactions_countIntegerNumber of supportive reaction taps.
flagged_for_moderationBooleanTrue if content requires safety review.
created_atTimestampPost creation timestamp.
4 Daily Motivational Checklist (DailyChecklistTask)
Habit Formation

A structured daily self-care habit or positive affirmation prompt for user well-being.

Why store it: Implements FR-09 to help users build positive daily momentum and track small self-care wins.

Field NameData TypeDescription & Example
task_idUUIDUnique task item identifier.
user_idUUIDForeign key to Patient Profile.
titleString"Drink 2 glasses of water", "Repeat daily affirmation", "5-min mindful breathing".
categoryEnumHYDRATION, MINDFULNESS, AFFIRMATION, PHYSICAL_MOVEMENT.
is_completedBooleanTrue if checked off today.
completed_atTimestampCompletion timestamp.
5 Thought & Gratitude Journal (JournalEntry)
Private Journaling

A private, encrypted personal journal entry written by the user in OurPadi.

Why store it: Implements FR-13 to enable emotional release, cognitive reframing, and gratitude reflection.

Field NameData TypeDescription & Example
journal_idUUIDUnique journal entry identifier.
user_idUUIDForeign key to Patient Profile.
entry_titleStringHeading or prompt title (e.g. "Late Night Generator Anxiety").
body_textTextEncrypted private reflection text.
entry_typeEnumFREEFORM, GRATITUDE_PROMPT, COGNITIVE_REFRAME, VENTING.
sentiment_tagEnumCALM, HEAVY, FRUSTRATED, HOPEFUL.
is_private_encryptedBooleanTrue to enforce client-side encryption.
created_atTimestampEntry creation timestamp.
6 Mental Health Resource (ResourceLibraryArticle)
Psychoeducation Library

A curated psychoeducation article, self-care guide, or crisis coping strategy in OurPadi.

Why store it: Implements FR-14 to give users quick access to evidence-based mental health education.

Field NameData TypeDescription & Example
article_idUUIDUnique article identifier.
titleStringTitle (e.g. "How to Ground Yourself During a Sudden Panic Attack").
topic_tagEnumPANIC_DISORDER, DEPRESSION_COPING, PARENTAL_TRAUMA, ADDICTION_TRIGGERS.
estimated_read_time_minsIntegerEstimated reading duration (e.g. 3).
content_markdownTextFormatted psychoeducational content.
author_clinician_idUUIDForeign key to vetting clinician.
7 ASMR & Calming Soundscape (ASMRTrack)
Audio Relaxation

A curated high-quality audio file designed for nocturnal decompression, relaxation, and sleep aid.

Why store it: Implements FR-11 to provide non-verbal somatic calming tools during midnight stress.

Field NameData TypeDescription & Example
track_idUUIDUnique audio track identifier.
titleString"Midnight Abuja Rainstorm", "Gentle Ocean Swell", "Whispered Affirmations".
categoryEnumNATURE_SOUNDS, WHISPERED_ASMR, BINAURAL_BEATS, WHITE_NOISE.
duration_secondsIntegerTrack duration (e.g. 1800 for 30 minutes).
audio_stream_urlStringLow-bandwidth audio stream URL.
8 Mood Check-in & Shift Log (MoodLog)
Self-Tracking

Continuous mood check-in entries and post-shift reflection records.

Why store it: Implements FR-08 to track emotional valence throughout the day and correlate with triggers.

Field NameData TypeDescription & Example
mood_log_idUUIDUnique mood entry identifier.
user_idUUIDForeign key to Patient Profile.
mood_ratingEnumRELIEVED, EXHAUSTED, ANXIOUS, DEFEATED, HOPEFUL.
stress_triggersArray[String]Tags (e.g. ["POWER_BLACKOUT", "CLIENT_REJECTION", "PARENTAL_STRAIN"]).
logged_atTimestampLog timestamp.
9 Standardized Assessment (ClinicalAssessment)
Longitudinal Telemetry

Standardized mental health screenings completed in OurPadi (WHO-5, GAD-7, PHQ-9).

Why store it: Powers the Prediction → Prevention engine and Clinician RPM Dashboard.

Field NameData TypeDescription & Example
assessment_idUUIDUnique assessment record identifier.
user_idUUIDForeign key to Patient Profile.
assessment_typeEnumWHO_5_WELLBEING, GAD_7_ANXIETY, PHQ_9_DEPRESSION.
raw_scoreIntegerRaw total score (e.g. 9 out of 25).
normalized_scoreFloatStandardized 0–100 scale score (e.g. 36.0).
severity_categoryEnumOPTIMAL, MILD_STRAIN, MODERATE_DEPRESSION, SEVERE_ANXIETY.
submitted_atTimestampIntake completion timestamp.
