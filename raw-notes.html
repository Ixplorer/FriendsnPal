The 6 Forcing Questions Deep-Dive

1. Who EXACTLY is the user?

The Real Human: A 24-year-old Upwork copywriter living in Garki, Abuja. She works US graveyard shifts through erratic power outages and generator noise. She is the sole caretaker for her younger brother—a sickle-cell warrior. She is wrestling with a failing relationship, severe sleep deprivation, and mounting medical bills. When she burns out, she loses clients and survival income.

2. How do you know this is real?

The Evidence: In Nigeria’s remote gig economy, there is zero safety net—no health insurance, no sick leave. A 3:00 AM panic attack directly triggers a missed Upwork deadline, a 1-star review, contract termination, and inability to purchase hydroxyurea and pain meds for her brother.

3. What happens if FriendnPal does not exist?

The Failure State: She isolates, cries alone in the dark, tries to type incoherently into ChatGPT, misses her morning client delivery, and spirals into acute depression under crushing financial pressure.

4. Why FriendnPal over ChatGPT?

The Differentiator: ChatGPT is not clinically structured for panic triage (lacks WHO-5/PHQ-9 micro-assessment), gives cold corporate disclaimers ("I am an AI, consult a healthcare provider"), has no cultural fluency for Nigerian socioeconomic stressors, and cannot automatically triage or escalate to a vetted human therapist when suicide or severe crisis is flagged.

5. The 5,000 NGN Wallet Test

Willingness to Pay: 5,000 NGN (~$3.50) is accessible to an active foreign-gig freelancer, provided it is sold as income & deadline protection rather than an abstract "wellness app". Safeguarding a $500 monthly retainer for 5,000 NGN is high ROI.

6. Fatal Assumptions Corrected

Economic Realignment: You cannot provide human therapists inside a 5,000 NGN ($3.50) monthly subscription. FriendnPal must operate as a two-tier model: 5,000 NGN/mo for unlimited AI triage, with human sessions priced separately per session.

The Reframed Core Triad

01

The Customer (ICP)

The Solitary High-Stakes Remote Worker. 22–29 year old gig workers (copywriters, devs, designers) working night hours in Nigerian cities with dependent sick family members and no social safety net.

02

The Problem

Acute 3:00 AM panic and burnout episodes that threaten client deliveries and lifeline dollar revenue, in an environment where friends/prayer lack clinical tools and therapy is unaffordable.

03

The Value Proposition

A low-bandwidth 24/7 WhatsApp emergency emotional stabilizer that delivers 10-minute clinical micro-de-escalations to save the workday, with instant escalation to vetted human therapists for critical risk.

Immediate Product Guardrails

  • 1
    Kill 32 Languages & Voice for Launch

    Restrict the MVP to English and Nigerian Pidgin on WhatsApp text. Solve the 3:00 AM crisis for 100 Garki/Lagos freelancers before building more dialects.

  • 2
    Lock Tiered Pricing

    5,000 NGN/month for unlimited AI triage. Human clinical escalation billed pay-per-session (7,500 – 15,000 NGN) to protect margins.

  • 3
    Targeted Freelance Community Wedge

    Acquire through Upwork Nigeria groups, tech bootcamps, and remote freelancer communities instead of generic social media ads.
