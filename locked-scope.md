## 1. Locked MoSCoW Prioritization Table

| # | Feature | Description | MoSCoW Category |
| :- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **OurPadi Mobile Shell & Triage Chat (Screens 1 & 4)** | Cross-platform client with biometric/local login, taskbar navigation (`[Home]`, `[Communities]`, `[Companion]`, `[Journals]`, `[Resources]`), AI companion in Nigerian English/Pidgin, and explicit 2-button triage routing (`[Calm My Panic]` vs `[Rescue My Deadline]`). | **Must Have (#1 Priority)** |
| **2** | **Acute Somatic Reset & Exercises (Screen 5)** | Visual 4-4-4 box breathing gauge with animated expanding/contracting ring, device haptics, 5-4-3-2-1 room sensory grounding, and 1–5 distress rating slider. | **Must Have** |
| **3** | **Emergency Crisis Handoff Protocol (Screen 7)** | Hybrid safety engine scanning for danger keywords, direct confirmation check (`FD-02`), automated chat lock, and red emergency card serving direct WhatsApp link (`wa.me/...`) and phone hotline (`FD-01`). | **Must Have** |
| **4** | **Workday Rescue 15-Minute Sprint Engine (Screen 6)** | Operational deadline recovery tool that breaks chaotic deliverables into structured 15-minute micro-sprints with a digital countdown timer and draft submission box. | **Must Have** |
| **5** | **Clinical Assessment Intake Engine (WHO-5)** | Standardized 5-question WHO-5 Well-Being Index questionnaire with Likert selection cards (0–5), automated score normalization (0–100), and backend telemetry dispatch. | **Must Have** |
| **6** | **Clinician RPM Caseload & Alert Dashboard (Screen 8)** | Secure web portal for licensed therapists displaying patient cohort metrics, real-time risk tier badges (`NORMAL`, `MODERATE`, `CRITICAL`), and audio-visual emergency alert banners. | **Must Have** |
