Clinical Methodology & Ecosystem

Inverting Mental Health Care from Reactive Crisis to Proactive Prevention

Traditional mental healthcare operates on a broken model (*"Symptoms → Crisis → Emergency Hospitalization"*). FriendnPal replaces this with continuous longitudinal screening, AI companionship, and real-time remote patient monitoring.

The Core Paradigm: Prediction → Prevention → Proactive Intervention

By measuring behavioral signals and clinical screening scores (WHO-5, GAD-7) continuously, the platform intercepts psychological distress before it escalates into an acute emergency.

1
PREDICTION — Longitudinal Screening & Score Trajectory

Patients complete 2-minute WHO-5 Well-Being screening assessments. Our analytics engine detects score drops (>30% decline over 14 days) and triggers early warnings.

2
PREVENTION — Micro-Interventions & Somatic Resets

Delivers instant 4-4-4 box breathing exercises, 5-4-3-2-1 sensory room grounding, ASMR soundscapes, and 15-minute Workday Rescue sprints to break acute task paralysis.

3
PROACTIVE INTERVENTION — Hybrid Clinician RPM & WhatsApp Handoff

When high-risk distress occurs, the system locks chat and serves direct 1-tap WhatsApp counselor access (`wa.me/...`) and toll-free hotlines while dispatching urgent alerts to Clinician RPM.

🇳🇬

Validation Beachhead: Nocturnal Freelancers

Our high-stress benchmark persona is Amina, a 24-year-old nocturnal Upwork copywriter in Garki, Abuja. Facing nocturnal US shifts (10:00 PM – 6:00 AM WAT), erratic power blackouts, generator noise, and family caregiving duties, she experiences severe deadline panic.

"If our de-escalation engine can stabilize an isolated gig worker at 3:00 AM under blackouts, it effortlessly serves corporate employees, students, and wellness patients."
