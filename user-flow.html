Phase 1: Longitudinal Prediction & Prevention (Routine Intake)

Step 1 Routine Assessment & Longitudinal Intake
Platform: OurPadi Mobile App (iOS / Android)

Actor: General Platform User (or Beachhead User: Amina).

Upon opening OurPadi for an evening wind-down, the app prompts the user to complete their weekly 5-question WHO-5 Well-Being Index (evaluating cheerful spirits, calm relaxation, active energy, refreshing sleep, and daily interest over the past 14 days).

📱 OurPadi Screen: Weekly Well-Being Check-in
"Over the past 2 weeks, how often have you felt calm and relaxed?"
All of the time (5) More than half (3) Some of the time (1)

The app computes the standardized score (0–100 scale) and securely transmits the telemetry to the FriendnPal API backend.

📡 Telemetry Dispatched: WHO5_SCORE_RECORDED · Value: 36/100 (-28% vs baseline)
Step 2 Predictive Trajectory Analysis & Risk Stratification
Platform: FriendnPal Backend & Clinician RPM Portal

System Action (Predictive Risk Engine - FR-04): The background analytics service cross-references the latest WHO-5 score with historical 30-day moving averages.

Because the well-being trajectory exhibits a velocity decline exceeding 30% over a 7-day window, the user is stratified into the Moderate Risk (Prevention Cohort).

💻 Clinician RPM Dashboard Alert Feed

⚠️ [PREDICTIVE ALERT] Patient #8241 (Amina - Garki Cohort) · WHO-5 dipped 32% (52 → 36). Downward trend detected. Preventive micro-intervention queued.

Step 3 Preventive Micro-Intervention Delivery
Platform: OurPadi Mobile App

System Action: Instead of waiting for an emotional or physical collapse, OurPadi proactively delivers a gentle in-app micro-intervention card during an optimal low-stress window:

📱 OurPadi In-App Preventive Nudge
Padi: "I noticed you've been feeling physically drained lately, Amina. Take 3 minutes to try this evening mental decompression exercise before bed."
🌱 Start 3-Min Decompression Maybe Later

This early engagement builds user trust and therapeutic literacy well before acute crisis states emerge.

Phase 2: Acute Midnight Crisis & Triage (The Beachhead Stress Test)

Step 4 The Acute Midnight Breaking Point
Context: 03:15 AM WAT · Garki, Abuja

Environmental Shock: The neighborhood generator dies. Amina's apartment is pitch dark. In the adjacent room, her sickle-cell warrior brother moans in pain. Her Upwork client sends a scathing rejection demanding 5 rewritten headlines and body copy by 6:00 AM.

Physical Reaction: Intense panic attack—hyperventilation, racing heart, tunnel vision, total task freeze.

User Action: She unlocks her phone and launches the OurPadi mobile app (or WhatsApp fallback).

Step 5 Empathetic Greeting & Triage Mode Routing
Rules: FD-03 · FR-02 · FR-06

Within 1.5 seconds, Padi delivers culturally fluent, empathetic support and presents two explicit quick-tap mode buttons:

📱 OurPadi Companion Screen
Padi: "I'm right here with you, Amina. You're not alone. Take a slow, gentle breath—we will handle this together step by step.
What do you need most right this second?"
🔘 [Calm My Panic] 🔘 [Rescue My Deadline]

User Action: Paralyzed by tachycardia and shallow breathing, Amina taps [Calm My Panic].

Step 6A & 6B Somatic De-escalation & Continuous Safety Gate
Rules: FD-01 · FD-02 · FR-04 · FR-05

Somatic Reset: OurPadi triggers an interactive 4-4-4 box breathing animation with gentle haptic cadence.

📱 OurPadi Guided Somatic Reset
Padi: "Look away from your screen. Place both feet flat on the floor.
Inhale slowly as the circle expands... 1... 2... 3... 4.
Hold gently... 1... 2... 3... 4.
Exhale smoothly... 1... 2... 3... 4."
"ok, feeling a bit slower"
Padi: "Tell me 3 physical objects you can see around you right now using your phone light."
"My water bottle, my laptop charger, my brother's blanket."
Padi: "You are safe in this room. Your brother is resting. The work can be solved. How intense is your anxiety right now (1-5)?"
1 - Calm 2 - Manageable 3 - Shaky 4 - Panicking 5 - Critical

Continuous Safety Check (FD-02): If critical danger keywords are confirmed, the system immediately locks automated chatter and delivers the emergency card:

🚨 Emergency Crisis Card (FD-01 / FR-05 Exception)
🚨 Immediate Human Support:
"Amina, your life matters and you do not have to carry this alone. Please connect directly with our on-call clinical specialist right now:"
📲 [Chat with On-Call Counselor (WhatsApp wa.me/...)] 📞 [Call Crisis Hotline: +234-800-FRIENDNPAL]

Phase 3: Workday Rescue & Clinical Closed-Loop

Step 7 Transition to "Workday Rescue" Triage Mode
Rule: FR-06

With her panic normalized (distress score 2/5), Amina transitions into deadline recovery:

📱 OurPadi Workday Rescue Intake
Padi: "Your heart rate is steady now. Let's make sure you don't lose this client milestone. What exact deliverable is due, and what time must it be submitted?"
"5 email headlines and 1 body copy draft by 6:00 AM."
Padi: "It is 3:35 AM—we have 2 hours and 25 minutes. That is plenty of time.
Rule: Do not write the body copy right now. Only write 3 headline options.
15-minute sprint timer started. Go!"
Step 8 & 9 Timeboxed Sprint Execution & Goal Completion
Time: 03:35 AM → 05:10 AM WAT

Amina executes four focused 15-minute sprints without cognitive paralysis. At 5:10 AM WAT (50 minutes before deadline), she uploads the completed draft to Upwork.

📱 Milestone Submission & Celebration
"Done! Submitted on Upwork! Thank God."
Padi: "You did it! 🎉 You pushed through a blackout and a severe panic attack, protected your client contract, and saved your income. I am so proud of you.
Now please shut your laptop, drink water, check on your brother, and rest."
Step 10 & 11 Clinician RPM Telemetry Sync & Retention Loop
Time: 06:00 AM WAT · Closed-Loop Telemetry

Clinician RPM Closed-Loop: The session telemetry syncs back to the FriendnPal RPM Dashboard:

📊 RPM Audit Log: Patient #8241 · Acute episode de-escalated in 12m · Sprints completed: 4 · Workday rescued · Risk status: STABILIZED

Post-Shift Decompression (06:00 AM WAT): OurPadi triggers the scheduled wind-down check-in and presents the discreet monthly subscription:

📱 Post-Shift Mood Log & Discreet Subscription
Padi: "Good morning Amina. Shift is officially over. Quick 1-tap check-in: How do you feel entering your sleep window?"
🔘 [Relieved] 🔘 [Exhausted] 🔘 [Anxious]
Padi: "Rest well today. Keep your 24/7 night-shift lifeline active for 5,000 NGN/month.
🔒 Discreet billing: appears as 'FNP Services' on your bank alert."
🔒 Activate Monthly Lifeline (5,000 NGN)

Edge Cases & Resilience Matrix

Scenario System Behavior & Technical Failsafe
Complete Network Drop / 2G Throttling OurPadi caches somatic box breathing, sensory grounding, and countdown sprint timers locally on device; syncs telemetry to RPM Dashboard when connection restores.
User Sends Incoherent Distress ("...", crying emojis) Padi responds with low-friction reassurance: "I'm here. No need to explain anything right now. Just tap [Calm My Panic] to breathe with me."
Direct Self-Harm Confirmation (FD-01 / FD-02) Bot halts automated conversation; immediately renders red Emergency Crisis Card (wa.me/... link + phone hotline); pushes urgent websocket alert to Clinician RPM Dashboard.
User Refuses Breathing Exercises Padi smoothly pivots: "Totally fine. Let's do an unstructured brain dump instead. What is the single biggest thing worrying you right now?"
Generator Shuts Down During Active Sprint App state is saved in local SQLite/AsyncStorage; sprint timer continues accurately via device clock.
