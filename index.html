Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between OurPadi Mobile and FriendnPal Clinician RPM?
OurPadi Mobile is the patient companion app for continuous mood tracking, AI companion triage in Nigerian Pidgin & English, 4-4-4 box breathing, workday rescue sprints, and crisis support. FriendnPal Clinician RPM is the web dashboard where licensed psychiatrists and care teams monitor patient caseloads, score trajectories, and triage high-risk alerts.
How does the 24/7 WhatsApp emergency counselor handoff work?
When explicit danger keywords (e.g. self-harm or suicidal ideation) are detected or when the user taps the Emergency Shield (`🛡️ Crisis`), the app locks chat and serves a direct 1-tap WhatsApp link (`wa.me/...`) to an on-call human counselor while dispatching a high-priority alert to the Clinician RPM Dashboard.
How is my data protected and what appears on my bank statement?
All personal journals and health telemetry are encrypted using client-side AES encryption under NDPR guidelines. To preserve complete privacy and eliminate stigma, all subscription charges appear on your bank statement under the neutral name "FNP Services".
Is human therapy included in the 5,000 NGN monthly subscription?
The 5,000 NGN monthly subscription covers unlimited AI companion triage, WHO-5 tracking, somatic box breathing, workday rescue sprints, peer communities, and resource libraries. 1-on-1 human therapy sessions with MDCN-certified specialists are billed pay-per-session (7,500 – 15,000 NGN).
